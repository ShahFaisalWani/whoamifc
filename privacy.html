Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Who Am I FC ("we") makes the Guess the Player videos on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. This policy covers our accounts and the apps we use to publish them, including the "Who Am I FC Instagram" app.

What our apps do

Our apps are used only by us, on our own accounts (@whoamifcquiz). They:

  • publish our own videos to our own accounts;
  • write comments and replies on our own posts;
  • read the comments on our own posts, so we can count which players viewers guessed. We show those counts as percentages in our next video.

What we collect

We collect the text and time of the comments on our own posts. We don't collect or keep commenters' usernames, profile details, photos, messages, contact details or any other personal information. We don't use cookies, ads or tracking on this site.

How we use and keep it

Comment text is used only to count guesses for our videos. It's stored on our own computer, without usernames. Only the totals and percentages ever appear in a video. We never sell, rent or share comment data with anyone.

Deleting your data

To have a comment you wrote removed from our records, email whoamifcquiz@gmail.com with a link to the comment. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also delete the comment on the platform itself.

The platforms

Instagram, Facebook and YouTube handle your account data under their own privacy policies: Instagram, Meta and Google.

Children

Our apps don't knowingly collect anything from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For any question, email whoamifcquiz@gmail.com.
